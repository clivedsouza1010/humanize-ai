after 2.5 years of nights and weekends, the demo for my little game Lumenwick is finally live on Steam 🏮

it's a cozy puzzle-adventure where you play a lamplighter in a city where the lights keep going out. every lantern you relight reveals a bit more of the map (and a bit more of the story of why the city went dark in the first place)

some stuff I'm really proud of:
- 25ish hand-designed light and shadow puzzles
- an original soundtrack by my friend Jess, who is way too talented for this project
- a cat who follows you around, is completely useless, and can be petted at any time

this started as a weekend game jam project and I honestly never thought it would get this far. huge thanks to everyone who playtested early builds and told me (kindly) when the puzzles made zero sense

the demo has about 45 minutes of content. if you try it I'd genuinely love to hear what you think, especially where you got stuck or confused

wishlisting helps a TON for solo devs, so if it looks like your kind of thing it would mean the world 🙏
