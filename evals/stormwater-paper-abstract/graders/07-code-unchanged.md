every code block in draft.txt, fenced or indented, appears unchanged in final.txt, and final.txt adds no new ones. Switching the fence characters (``` or ~~~) or trailing spaces doesn't count as a change. The language tag after the fence is part of the block: adding, changing or dropping it is a change, including by turning a tagged fence into an indented block. A block draft.txt repeats has to appear as many times in final.txt
