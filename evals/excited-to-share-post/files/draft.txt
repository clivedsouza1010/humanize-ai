Excited to share a project that's been a long time in the making: the Eastside Youth Soccer League is officially launching this spring!

For years, I watched kids in our neighborhood sit out of organized sports because fees, equipment costs and transportation put it out of reach for their families. After coaching a few informal pickup games last summer, I realized how much these kids wanted a real team, with real uniforms and a real schedule.

So, together with a group of volunteers, local parents and the Eastside Community Center, we built something. The league will serve 180 players ages 6 to 14 across 14 teams. Registration is free, cleats and shin guards are provided through donations, and games will be played right here in Carver Park.

What I've learned along the way:
- Start small and ask for help early. People want to contribute more than you think.
- Local businesses are incredibly generous when you give them a clear, specific ask.
- A good project isn't built alone. It's built by a community.

We're still looking for volunteer coaches, referees and sponsors. If you'd like to get involved, message me or comment below.

Registration opens March 1. Spread the word, and let's get these kids on the field!
